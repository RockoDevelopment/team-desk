Doorline (Demo) Privacy Policy

Rocko Development LLC · Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy applies to the Doorline (Demo) Android app published by Rocko Development LLC. Doorline (Demo) is a clickable prototype so a union can try the canvassing workflow. It is not a production service and is not connected to a live server.

No accounts, and no data collection

The app does not offer account creation. A demo sign-in is pre-filled on the device so you can try the screens. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information through this demo, and we do not receive a copy of anything you type or tap in it.

What stays on your device

Everything in this demo stays on your device. The server, GPS, and teammates you see in the app are simulated locally. If you uninstall the app, that on-device demo data is removed with it.

Internet permission

The app declares the Android INTERNET permission. This demo does not use that permission to send your information to Rocko Development or to any third party.

Children

Doorline (Demo) is a workplace tool demo. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the demo can be sent to contact@rockodevelopment.com.
